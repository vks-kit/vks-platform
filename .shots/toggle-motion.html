Checkbox & Switch · press in / press out

Press and HOLD, then release. Drag off before releasing = cancel (no toggle). Try slow-mo.
NOW

Current build

  • Switch knob snaps. Tailwind v4 moves it with the translate property, but the CSS only transitions transform, so the slide never animates.
  • Knob stretch uses scale → the white circle squashes into an oval.
  • Checkbox: press shrink is OK, but the green fill just fades in; no feedback on the label; release has no "landing".
PROPOSED

v0.4 press in → release

  • Press in (90-140 ms): checkbox sinks to 86 %, a soft green halo appears, border turns green. Switch knob stretches into a pill toward where it will go; off-track darkens a touch. Label dims to 60 %.
  • Release: checkbox fill grows from the centre with a spring, tick draws right after, box bounces back past 100 %, halo ripples out once. Switch knob springs across (slight overshoot) while shrinking back to a circle; green floods out from the middle of the track; one ring pulse.
  • Uncheck is quicker and quieter: tick un-draws, fill shrinks, no ripple.
  • Transform + opacity only · ≤ 460 ms · 4 ms haptic on release · lite mode drops halo/ring/glow.
